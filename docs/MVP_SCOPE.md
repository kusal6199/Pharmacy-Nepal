# MVP scope

## Included

1. Medicine and product master
2. Batch and expiry inventory
3. Fast keyboard-oriented POS
4. Purchase entry
5. Sales and purchase returns
6. Expiry and low-stock alerts
7. Configurable Nepal invoice, PAN, and tax fields
8. Supplier, customer, and credit balances
9. Core operational reports
10. Users, roles, and audit trail

## Deferred

- Multi-branch and wholesale workflows
- Cloud synchronization
- Full general-ledger accounting and payroll
- E-commerce, mobile apps, and online ordering
- Loyalty and marketing automation
- AI forecasting and prescription OCR
- CBMS integration until a pilot customer establishes the exact requirement

## Pilot completion criteria

A pilot pharmacy can complete a working day without internet: receive a batch, sell pack or loose units, print a valid invoice, process a controlled return, reconcile stock, review expiry/low-stock warnings, and back up the database.
